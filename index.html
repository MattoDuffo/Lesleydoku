How to play

  • Place exactly one bunny in every row, every column and every coloured patch.
  • No two bunnies may touch — not even corner to corner.
  • Tap a cell once to rule it out, again to place a bunny, again to clear it. Drag to rule out a run of cells.
  • Every puzzle is solvable by logic alone. You never need to guess.
  • Stuck? Hint points at the row, column or patch that gives the next move. Press it again for the reasoning, and once more to play it.
